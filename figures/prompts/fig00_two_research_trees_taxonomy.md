## Required reading structure

Two trees side by side: "AI for Data Prep" on the left, "Data Prep for AI" on the right. Each tree has exactly SIX distinct topical branch compartments, arranged as two columns by three tiers. Each compartment is a generously spaced rounded botanical lobe with a thin outline, very pale color fill, a bold subfield heading, and its OWN visible upward stem. Connect every paper in a compartment to THAT stem using short twigs. Connect the base of that stem to its parent tree trunk using a single curved branch. A compartment contains ONE subfield only. No twig, node or subfield stem connects to any other compartment. The grouping must be obvious even without reading paper titles. This is a taxonomy first, a chronological overview within each category second.

Inside EACH compartment, stack the exact paper labels in the exact TOP-TO-BOTTOM order specified below (newest at top, oldest at bottom). Never reverse this order. This is LOCAL chronology: do not add a global year axis, horizontal year grid, or suggest that the heights of separate compartments are comparable. Do not scatter one subfield's papers across several compartments. Do not mix categories to create global year rows.

Use a tall portrait page, roughly 3:4, highest supported native resolution. Four equal text columns overall, two per tree. Reserve enough width for labels to wrap across two lines without tiny type. Use a restrained botanical scientific-illustration style, ample contrast, clean near-vector lines. Keep decorative foliage small and outside text. All text lies on fully opaque white or very pale solid fill, never transparent.

Layout guide on a normalized 1086 x 1448 canvas:
- Title "Research landscape" near y=28, followed by the two tree titles near y=85.
- Four compartment columns spanning approximately x=20–250, 295–525, 560–790, 835–1065. Left tree trunk in the gap near x=272; right near x=812.
- Top tier approximately y=120–420; middle y=440–740; bottom y=760–1100. Compartment heights may vary slightly to accommodate long labels, but must not overlap.
- Tree trunks join fine roots at y=1140, below the six topical compartments. Root connectors meet a shared neutral-gray foundations compartment centered below both trees, y=1185–1390. The roots must not pass through any label.
- Compact footnote at very bottom. Do not waste space on repeated bottom tree titles.
